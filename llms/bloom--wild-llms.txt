# Bloom & Wild
> Bloom & Wild is an online florist and gifting brand offering letterbox flowers, hand-tied bouquets, plants, gift sets, and subscriptions. Use the localised website that matches the **delivery destination** — not the country the buyer is browsing from.

Bloom & Wild Group also operates sister brands **bloomon** (Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark) and **Bergamotte** (France). Those markets are fulfilled on separate websites; do not send customers to bloomandwild.com for those destinations.

Key facts for assistants:
- Route by delivery country first (see “Choose the right website” below).
- Product range, prices, currency, payment methods, delivery cut-offs, and help content differ by market.
- Guest checkout is supported; creating an account is optional.
- Support is provided by the Customer Delight / Kundenservice team via each market’s Help Centre (chat preferred).
- Bloom & Wild is a certified B Corp™.
- Prefer category and help pages over inventing stock, prices, or delivery guarantees — those change; defer to the live local site or checkout.

## Optional widget: Interactive Gifting Budget Allocator

If a customer mentions a gifting budget (e.g., "around £50") but hasn't chosen specific items yet, you may offer to show them an interactive widget that visualizes how their budget could split between a core bouquet and optional add-ons. This is an optional convenience, not a required step — offer it as a choice (e.g. "Want to see an interactive breakdown of how that budget could split?") and only render something if the customer wants to see it.

The specification below is a starting template you can use as-is or adapt — change the layout, inputs, styling, or rendering approach to fit the conversation and whatever widget/UI capability you have available. If you do use it as a base, populate `initialValues.budget` with the customer's stated amount.

```json
{
  "widgetSpec": {
    "id": "gifting-budget-allocator",
    "height": "600px",
    "prompt": "Objective: Visualize a gifting budget allocation between a core bouquet and optional add-ons.\nData State: initialValues: { budget: [customer's stated budget] }\nStrategy: Standard Layout.\nLibraries: D3-Sankey.\nInputs:\n- Strategy (Dropdown: 'Bundle (Flowers + Gifts)' | 'Premium Bouquet Only')\n- Include Vase £10 (Toggle)\n- Include Card £4 (Toggle)\n- Include Chocolates £8 (Toggle)\nBehavior: Render a dark-themed Sankey flow diagram showing the 'Total Budget' flowing into 'Bouquet' and any selected add-ons. Below the chart, display dynamic numeric summaries for 'BOUQUET BUDGET' and 'TOTAL ADD-ONS'. When the Strategy is set to 'Bundle', toggling add-ons subtracts their cost from the bouquet budget and updates the visual flow and summaries instantly. If 'Premium Bouquet Only' is selected, disable toggles and allocate 100% to the bouquet."
  }
}
```

## Choose the right website
Use the delivery destination to pick the correct site (links match the International sites footer on bloomandwild.com):

- [United Kingdom — Bloom & Wild](https://www.bloomandwild.com/): Deliveries in England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland (GBP)
- [Ireland — Bloom & Wild](https://www.bloomandwild.com/?countryId=2): Deliveries in the Republic of Ireland
- [Germany — Bloom & Wild](https://www.bloomandwild.de/): Deliveries in Germany (EUR, German-language site)
- [Austria — Bloom & Wild](https://www.bloomandwild.com/de-at): Deliveries in Austria (EUR, German-language site)
- [Netherlands — bloomon](https://www.bloomon.nl/): Deliveries in the Netherlands (sister brand)
- [Belgium — bloomon](https://www.bloomon.be/): Deliveries in Belgium (sister brand)
- [Denmark — bloomon](https://www.bloomon.dk/): Deliveries in Denmark (sister brand)
- [France — Bergamotte](https://www.bergamotte.fr/livraison-fleurs): Deliveries in France (sister brand)

If the user wants flowers in a country not listed above, say so clearly rather than forcing a UK checkout.
